# Product photos

All product photos live in `p/` as 800×1000 WebP files, framed around the
product. `data/product-images.json` maps each product slug to its file.

| Source | Products | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parfum City listing on Wolt — https://wolt.com/az/aze/baku/venue/parfum-city | 337 | The boutique's own studio photos of its stock. |
| Official brand websites | 149 | Studio packshots from tomfordbeauty.com, xerjoff.com, rojaparfums.com, thameenlondon.com, vilhelmparfumerie.com, memoparis.com, scentologia.com, widian.com, en.zilli.com, yslbeauty.com and prada-beauty.com. |

The photos belong to the boutique and to the respective brands. They are used
here only to present this design concept and must be replaced or licensed
before any public launch.

Products for which neither source had a usable studio photo were removed from
the catalogue (see the README).
